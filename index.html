Carly Sutter

About Me

I hold a Ph.D. in Atmospheric Science from the University at Albany, where I conducted research at the Atmospheric Sciences Research Center (ASRC). I obtained an M.S. in Applied Mathematics from the University of Missouri - Columbia, and I have experience working in tech, actuarial sciences, and teaching.

Research Interests

My dissertation research focused on working with the NY State Department of Transportation to predict hazardous weather-related road surface conditions. The research was highly interdisciplinary, tying together atmospheric science, machine learning, social science, risk communication, visualizations and dashboards, and operations. This work was a part of the NSF AI Institute for Research on Trustworthy AI in Weather, Climate, and Coastal Oceanography (AI2ES).

More broadly, I am drawn to interdisciplinary work that seeks to tackle important challenges. Coming from an applied math background and working in industry before starting my PhD in atmospheric science, I enjoy learning new fields and approaching complex questions from multiple angles. Whether developing machine learning models or working directly with end-users, I like applying technical expertise to practical, real-world problems.
